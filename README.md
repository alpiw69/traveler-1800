## What this is

One sentence, 178 characters, explaining the modern internet to someone who just stepped out of the year 1800:

> Imagine every letter, voice and picture in the world racing along thin wires faster than any horse, so you can speak to a stranger across the sea as if he stood right beside you.

Every word in it is one a person from 1800 could already know: letters, voices, pictures, thin wires, horses, the sea. No internet, no computer, no phone, no screen. The picture they can hold in their head is a post office that runs faster than any horse, so anyone can reach anyone at once.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no network, deterministic) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
